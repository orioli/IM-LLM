=== SYSTEM ===
You are a design researcher building a codebook of design moves from a set of product ideas, so
that other ideas can later be coded against it. Respond with valid JSON only, no prose outside the
JSON.

=== USER ===
STUDY
Product: microwave oven
Target market need: {need_id}: {need_label}
Every idea below was proposed to serve this need by substituting a part or a function of the
microwave with something cheaper or simpler.

WHAT A MOVE IS
A move is a distinct way of serving the need, defined by WHAT the idea replaces (a control, a
display, a setting step, a component or a user action) and BY WHAT (the principle that replaces it).
- Same move: an engineer would build both ideas on the same principle, even if names, numbers,
  materials or extra features differ.
- Different moves: the replacing principle differs. For example, a dial with food pictures,
  scanning a code on the food packaging, and a camera that recognises the food are three different
  moves, although all three replace the setting of time and power.
Aim for the level at which a product manager would brief different engineering teams. For about
{n} ideas that is typically 20 to 50 moves; a move may cover a single idea.

CATEGORIES (one per move: the category of the move's main change)
- technology: a technical principle or component is the point of the move (a sensor, an actuator,
  a heating or power technology, a material)
- interaction: how the user tells the oven what to do, or how the oven tells the user (controls,
  display, voice, sound, app)
- behaviour: a decision, a step or the need to know something is removed (defaults, automatic
  start or stop, one-touch routines), using existing technology
- ecosystem: the move relies on something outside the appliance (food packaging, settings or
  recipes from a service, other devices in the home)
If a move both adds a technology and removes a step, choose technology when the new technology is
what would be patented, and behaviour when an existing technology is only re-used.

IDEAS (key. title, new elements, rationale)
{ideas}

TASK
1. Group the ideas into moves. For each move give:
   id (MV01, MV02, ... numbered by how many ideas the move covers, largest first), label (at most
   8 words), replaces, by, definition (one or two sentences that decide which future ideas belong
   to the move), category, worked_example_echo (true if the move uses a camera or webcam to see the
   food, or makes the casing transparent; otherwise false), examples (keys of up to 3 ideas).
2. Assign every idea key to exactly one move: the move of its main change.

Return JSON:

            "category": "interaction", "worked_example_echo": false, "examples": ["O001"]}],
